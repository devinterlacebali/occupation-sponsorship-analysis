Salary range by occupation area
Horizontal bars show the typical range (min–max median). The dark line marks the median mid-point. Only occupations with enough salary data are shown.
Occupation area Salary range (AUD, annualised) Median mid Records
How this is calculated: listings come in with per-hour, per-day, per-week or per-year rates. We annualise each one (38-hour week, 52 weeks for full-time equivalence) and apply sanity caps ($25k–$800k) before computing medians. This lets us compare, for example, an hourly warehouse role fairly against a salaried finance role.

Skills in Demand — by Occupation

Skill libraries are tailored to each occupation: technical, certification & soft skills
Overall top skills across all listings
Aggregated from occupation-specific scans. Reflects both soft skills (common to all roles) and role-specific requirements.
Skills required for a specific occupation
Each occupation uses its own skill dictionary. E.g. IT & Technology looks for Python/AWS/Docker; Healthcare looks for AHPRA, WWCC & CPR; Construction looks for White Card, Forklift Licence.
Why occupation-specific? Platform-provided skill tags are missing on Indeed and inconsistent elsewhere. For each occupation we built a dedicated dictionary of hard skills (e.g. AutoCAD for Engineering, Xero for Finance) and soft skills — then scanned the full job descriptions. Tags next to each skill tell you whether it's a technical/certification requirement HARD or an interpersonal trait SOFT.

Experience level by occupation area
Choose an occupation to compare the mix of levels employers are seeking.
How levels are classified: we scan titles for keywords (senior, lead, manager, graduate, intern…) and parse descriptions for phrases like “5+ years of experience”, “training provided”, or “entry level”. This gives a consistent signal across Jora, Seek, and Indeed where the platform-provided level field is inconsistent or missing.
